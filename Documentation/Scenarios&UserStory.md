# Scenarios

## 1. Uni Student
John is a 1st year interstate uni student. Newly moved in and never been out of home, he is feeling stressed, lonely, and isolated.

He downloaded the app for more structure in life. He likes the budget goals, friendship missions, and study tracker. He builds a routine which makes him feel more comfortable.

---

## 2. First-time Mum
Maria is a first-time mum of a 2-year-old, Dave. She feels like she only focuses on family and has lost herself. She wants to reclaim her life: friends, fitness, and work.

She downloaded the app to help track her progress in improving her life. She tracks when she goes out with friends or goes to the gym. She journals her feelings and goals, such as career goals. She feels like she has more control over life, as she can visualise the changes she's making.

---

## 3. Burn-out 9-5 Worker
Alex is a 9-5 worker in IT and feels drained from having the same monotonous routine. He feels like something is missing but isn't sure what. He has free time and money but no passions.

He downloaded the app to figure out what in his life is unbalanced. The app identifies he needs to focus more on spirituality. He journals in the app according to prompts provided, which lead to vague questions related to "interests". After self-reflection, he rekindles interests. The app encourages Alex to try out his interests.

---

# User Stories

## Uni Student
- As a uni student, I want to be able to log in so I can save my progress  
- As a uni student, I want a budget tracker so I can track my expenses  
- As a uni student, I want weekly missions so I have more guidance on what to do  
- As a uni student, I want a study tracker so I can record my study sessions  
- As a uni student, I want to track my engagement with friends so I can maintain my relationships  
- As a uni student, I want a streak so I can feel motivated to keep going  
- As a uni student, I want the app to prompt me to catch up with friends so that I am reminded to meet up with them  
- As a uni student, I want a social feature in the app so I can see how my friends are going  

---

## First-time Mum
- As a first-time mum, I want an entry quiz so I can gauge where I am at  
- As a first-time mum, I want a physical activity tracker so I can be more active  
- As a first-time mum, I want a history log so I can review and see my progress  
- As a first-time mum, I want a journal feature so I can record my feelings and goals  
- As a first-time mum, I want a mood tracker so I can see how I felt over the year  
- As a first-time mum, I want a chore/to-do list so I can keep track of my daily and weekly tasks  

---

## 9-5 Worker
- As a 9-5 worker, I want to receive prompts so that I can reflect on my life  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want a balance wheel/visual so I can understand the status of each area  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want the app to give me suggestions so I can perform actionable steps to improve my life  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want the ability to take pictures of my newfound hobbies  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want the app to inform me of my sleeping habits  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want a diet and calorie tracker so I can see when I am eating healthy  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want each of the 5 domains to have a score out of 10 or 100 so I can track my individual progress  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want there to be a balance score so I can see how balanced my life is  
- As a 9-5 worker, I want a simplistic UI so that it is easy to navigate
